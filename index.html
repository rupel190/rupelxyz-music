Music

Things I built for playing, learning and listening.

  • Fretboard Mode Lab web tool · in your browser

    Guitar modes are kinda intimidating, so I created a tool to help understand them.

  • Songmap web tool · in your browser

    Load a track and map how it's built: waveform, bar grid, section markers and arrangement lanes. Runs in the browser, nothing is uploaded.

  • TUBS Renderer Obsidian plugin · demo

    Guitar TABS for drums. TUBS is an existing concept. The plugin enables you to render it nicely and play your patterns inside Obsidian. Try it here first.

  • Singify Spotify extension · write-up

    SingStar (karaoke) but for Spotify, with charts downloaded automatically and pitch scoring off your mic.
